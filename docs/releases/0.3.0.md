# Digiface 0.3.0

Draw pixel-art glyphs and turn them into a TrueType font (`.ttf`) for your game (GB Studio and other engines) and for design work that needs a crisp pixel typeface.

Digiface has two tools:

- **Digiface Matrix** (`web/index.html`, no install, works offline in a browser): makes the template to draw on, and, new in this version, lets you **draw the glyphs right in the page**.
- **Digiface for Aseprite** (`digiface-0.3.0.aseprite-extension`): *File > Scripts > Digiface > Export as TTF...*

## What is new since 0.2.0

- **An editor in the page.** Press **Edit pixel font**: the settings fold away and the tools appear. Brush and eraser of 1 to 4 pixels, a **Width** tool that sets the width of each character (and shows the new value on the canvas), undo and redo, zoom and **Fit**, a hand tool (also while you hold Space), a *Stay in cell* option, a line with Shift, **Zoom on cell**, and a **rectangle selection** to move, cut, copy and paste pixels (the pixels float until you place them). The keys are in the *Shortcuts* tab of the help.
- **Image tools.** **Delete image** (with a warning, and undo brings the drawing back); a red **Reset** button next to the help that starts again; the **Drawing** button waits until something is drawn. The settings (*Guide*, *Cells*, *Spacing*) are in one collapsible card, and *Use the guide font's cell size* is a check box. They stay editable while you draw: the drawing follows its characters and the page asks before it cuts anything.
- **The font from the page.** **Font (TTF)** downloads the TrueType font straight from what you drew, with no Aseprite needed (the window names the characters that are left out for lack of drawing). Its writer is a port of the extension's, and a test checks that both give the same bytes.
- **What you draw stays in view**, and the **Drawing** button downloads it as a PNG, without any guide and with the settings of the font inside. Open that PNG in Aseprite and export the font with the extension, no template to hide. The **Template** button still gives the grid to draw on in Aseprite. Each download opens a small window that explains it and asks for the name of the font.
- **GB Studio mode.** Tick **GB Studio**: the GB Studio layout, 8 x 8 cells and 16 columns are fixed, and the template and the drawing are indexed PNGs in the palette of GB Studio's font sheets (the palest color is the background, the darkest the characters). The drawing is the font image GB Studio reads; the window also saves the `.json` that GB Studio needs for the euro sign, the curly quotes, the ellipsis and oe. You can tick it from inside the editor: the drawing follows its characters.
- **GB Studio tools.** **Switch** swaps two palette colors everywhere (the background and the drop shadows too). **Add shadow** adds a drop shadow layer under the writing: the color to copy, the shadow color, thickness and offset; a tile per shadow to change or delete it. The downloaded drawing is flattened, shadows included.
- **The drawing follows its characters** when the layout changes, and the page asks before it loses anything. It is kept while the page stays open.
- **A new look** (neon terminal, dark), in Source Sans 3 (SIL OFL), and a "Made by pixelPep" line.
- The extension counts the palette of GB Studio as pure colors, so a drawing made with it does not warn about stray colors.

## Install

Download `digiface-0.3.0.aseprite-extension` below and double-click it, or use *Edit > Preferences > Extensions > Add Extension* in Aseprite. Aseprite asks you to approve each file the script opens.

To use Digiface Matrix, download the source code zip of this release and open `web/index.html`.

## Workflow

1. Open Digiface Matrix, choose a character set, spacing and cell size.
2. Press **Edit pixel font** and draw (or download the **Template** and draw in Aseprite). For variable widths, use the **Width** tool, or paint pure magenta (`#FF00FF`) over the unused columns on the right of each glyph in Aseprite.
3. Press **Font (TTF)** to get the font, or download the **Drawing**, open it in Aseprite, and run *File > Scripts > Digiface > Export as TTF...* (its dialog lists problems with the row and column of each character: errors block, warnings do not).

For GB Studio, put the PNG of the drawing and its `.json` in the `assets/fonts` folder of your project.

## Known limits

- A drawing is lost when the page is reloaded: saving in the browser is planned. Download the drawing to keep it.
- The selection tool, drop shadows and Switch are new and have had less testing than the rest.
- A drawing hidden under a width marker is lost in the download (the cell is framed in yellow and the window warns).
- The TrueType font from the page is new: its checks are lighter than the extension's (it lists the characters without drawing and the hidden drawing). Drawing in Aseprite still goes through the extension.
- A PNG made here has not been tried inside GB Studio itself; it follows what GB Studio's source reads.
- No kerning and no hinting. Characters that were not drawn are left out of the font.
- Tried in Aseprite 1.3.16 on macOS and in Chrome. Other versions, browsers and systems have not been tested.
